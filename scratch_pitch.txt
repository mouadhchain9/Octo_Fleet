01 — PROBLEM
FDM Problem
Every day, makers, engineering labs, and small businesses use FDM 3D printers to create prototypes, functional parts, and small production runs. However, printer failures remain a common problem. Unexpected breakdowns, failed prints, material waste, and machine downtime can lead to lost time and higher costs.
Most users still rely on reactive maintenance, meaning they only address problems after a failure occurs. This happens because desktop 3D printers often lack monitoring tools, and users have little visibility into the condition of their printers while a print is running. 
As a result, makers, laboratories, and small businesses need a simple solution that can monitor printer health in real time, detect potential issues early, and help prevent failures before they happen. This would reduce downtime, minimize waste, and allow users to print with greater confidence and reliability.

04 — Innovation
Innovation
Octo-Fleet is the first 100% Algerian hybrid digital twin for predictive maintenance of FDM 3D printers. It combines live 3D visualization with smart failure detection inside a web browser. No installation & No firmware changes.
The system works from day one and gets smarter with every print. Low-cost hardware makes predictive maintenance accessible to labs, makerspaces, and small businesses. Alerts are reliable and easy to understand so no unnecessary warnings or confusing data.
With proven accuracy and less unplanned downtime, Octo-Fleet lets you print with confidence, save filament, and keep your machines running longer.
